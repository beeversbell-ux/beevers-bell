THE BEEVERS + BELL RECORD
Council decision record · Kingsville, Victoria

21 April 2026 Council decision

Maribyrnong City Council endorsed the final concept plans for Beevers Reserve and Bell Reserve. Seven councillors were present and all seven voted for the motion.

Motion and vote

Moved

Cr Elena Pereyra

· Wattle Ward · moved the motion to endorse the Beevers Reserve and Bell Reserve final concept plans.
Seconded

Cr Bernadette Thomas

· Sheoak Ward · seconded the motion.
Vote
Councillors Mohamed Semra, Bernadette Thomas, Samantha Meredith, Elena Pereyra, Pradeep Tiwari, Susan Yengi and Cuc Lam voted for the motion. The motion was carried.

What the decision did

Council endorsed the final concept plans.

The resolution endorsed the Beevers Reserve Final Concept Plan and the Bell Reserve Final Concept Plan, and referred both projects to the budget process for future funding consideration.

It did not finally close Coronation Street.

The Bell Reserve concept includes the proposed partial closure of Coronation Street. Council's April report states that a separate statutory road-closure process, including public notice and consideration of submissions, is required before a later Council decision.

What was asked later

At the 16 June 2026 Council meeting, residents asked councillors what information they had before the April vote. During her response, Cr Elena Pereyra referred to the master-plan context, continuing traffic work and meeting residents that afternoon.
