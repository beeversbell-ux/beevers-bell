# The Beevers + Bell Record

Source repository for https://beeversbell.com

The Beevers + Bell Record is a sourced resident record covering the Beevers Reserve and Bell Reserve consultation, the April 2026 Council decision, subsequent Public Question Time, traffic evidence and the ongoing Coronation Street process in Kingsville, Victoria.

On 21 April 2026, Maribyrnong City Council endorsed the Beevers Reserve and Bell Reserve final concept plans. The motion was moved by Cr Elena Pereyra, Wattle Ward, and seconded by Cr Bernadette Thomas, Sheoak Ward.

The published website links claims to Council reports, meeting records, consultation documents and meeting recordings.
